How It Works

Open gym courts rotate throughout the session so everyone gets equal time on court. Arrive any time during the window.

  • Arrive any time during the session window
  • Check in at the door — sign waiver on first visit
  • Join the rotation: winners stay, losers rotate
  • All skill levels welcome on open gym courts
  • Shuttlecocks provided (feather or nylon by session)

Court Rotation

Winners stay, challengers rotate in — a fair social format that keeps games moving and lets you meet every player in the room over the course of an evening.

Training vs. Open Gym

Training runs simultaneously with open gym on dedicated courts — structured activities, not pickup play. You'll always know what you're working on.

Who is training for?

Not just advanced players. We run skill-differentiated tracks so beginners and experienced athletes get equal value from the same session.

  • Beginners learning fundamentals in a low-pressure setting
  • Intermediate players breaking bad habits and building consistency
  • Advanced players sharpening tactical and competitive skills
  • Youth players with age-appropriate coaching and progression

Rules & Waiver

A few ground rules keep everyone safe, protect the facility, and ensure a great experience for the whole community.

👟 Court & Equipment

  • Non-marking athletic shoes required — no exceptions
  • No outdoor shoes on the court
  • Handle shuttlecocks and equipment with care
  • Do not drag rackets or throw equipment
  • Report damaged nets or equipment to a coordinator

🔄 Court Rotation

  • Winners stay, losers rotate — open gym standard
  • Games to 21 (rally scoring), cap at 30
  • Challengers must be waiting before game ends to rotate in
  • Doubles is default during busy sessions
  • Singles courts designated if space allows

🤝 Sportsmanship

  • Call your own faults honestly — we self-officiate
  • Respectful communication at all times
  • Encourage new players; patience with beginners expected
  • No excessive celebration or taunting
  • Disputes settled with a re-serve or replay

🛡️ Safety

  • Warm up before intense play to prevent injury
  • Yield to players on adjacent courts
  • Pick up loose shuttles from your court immediately
  • Minors under 18 require a signed parental waiver
  • Report any injuries to a session coordinator

📋 Check-In

  • All participants must sign in each session
  • Waiver required before first session — no exceptions
  • Payment collected at check-in
  • Guests must also complete a waiver

🏫 Facility

  • Respect the school facility — clean up after yourself
  • No food or colored drinks on the gym floor
  • Water bottles ok — keep them off the court surface
  • Stay in designated play areas only

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit — or your hundredth.

I've never played badminton. Is this for me?▾
Absolutely. We welcome true beginners and actively encourage newcomers. Open gym is relaxed and social — experienced players are happy to help first-timers learn. Want a more structured intro? Let us know when you arrive.
Do I need to bring my own racket?▾
Loaner rackets are available for players trying the sport for the first time. If you plan to attend regularly, we recommend investing in your own racket for comfort. We're happy to suggest options at any price point.
Who provides shuttlecocks? Can I bring my own?▾
Shuttlecocks are provided at all sessions as part of your session fee. The type (feather or nylon) may vary by session. You're welcome to bring your own — just check with the coordinator about which type is being used that day.
What's the difference between open gym and training?▾
Open gym is casual drop-in play with a court rotation system — think pickup basketball but badminton. Training is structured coaching with drills, technique work, and coach feedback on dedicated courts. Some sessions offer both simultaneously. Training may have a separate fee.
Can players under 18 participate?▾
Yes. Youth players are welcome. Players under 18 require a parental or guardian signature on the liability waiver before participating. A parent does not need to stay during the session, but must complete the waiver. Youth-specific training is offered when demand warrants it.
Is West Metro Badminton affiliated with Wayzata High School?▾
No. West Metro Badminton is an independently organized community program. We rent gym space at Wayzata High School but have no affiliation with the school's athletics department, staff, or administration. We are a separate, privately run community initiative.
What shoes do I need?▾
Non-marking athletic court shoes are required — lighter-colored rubber soles that don't scuff gym floors. Most running shoes or cross-trainers work fine. Outdoor shoes, cleats, or dark-soled shoes are not permitted. This rule is strictly enforced to protect the school's gym floor.
How do I find out about cancellations or schedule changes?▾
We communicate via email and social media. Follow our channels and sign up for email updates. We aim to announce cancellations at least 24 hours ahead — school event conflicts can occasionally be last-minute.
Can I come just to watch without playing?▾
Of course — come check it out! Non-players are welcome to observe from the sidelines. If you decide to join mid-session, just let the coordinator know. No obligation to play if you want to watch first.
